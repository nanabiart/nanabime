NEON
FESTIVAL NÔMADE

The project for Neon involved developing the visual concept for Festival Nômade from the initial concept through its final execution. For this project, I worked alongside the NOW team as an Art Direction Assistant, contributing to defining the visual language, including the direction of illustrations and graphic elements, ensuring the work was aligned with the brand guidelines and consistently reflected Neon’s identity. I also participated in the real-time coverage, developing carousel content published throughout the festival.
