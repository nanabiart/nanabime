JOHNNIE WALKER
LOLLAPALOOZA 2026

The objective for Johnnie Walker was to develop the brand’s visual communication for Lollapalooza 2026, creating a real-time coverage aligned with its identity and the festival’s dynamics. The strategy brought the Go-Go Highball territory and Sabrina Carpenter partnership into the festival context, connecting the product, the experience, and the brand.

For this project, I worked alongside the NOW team as an Art Direction Assistant, contributing to the planning and visual direction of the content. The planning aimed to translate the campaign territory into a relevant and consistent visual language throughout the festival. Keep Walking.

CIELO
LOLLAPALOOZA 2026

For Lollapalooza 2026, Cielo developed a visual direction designed to bring the brand closer to the audience and the energy of the festival. The strategy explored Brazilian identity through the universe of boteco and gatherings, creating a relaxed, diverse, playful, and warm visual language.

For this project, I worked alongside the NOW team as an Art Direction Assistant, contributing to the planning and development of the visual concept. The planning transformed everyday elements of Brazilian culture into a contemporary and spontaneous visual language connected to the festival experience.
