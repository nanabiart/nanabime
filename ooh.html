ANIMALE
INVERNO

Out-of-home adaptation for ANIMALE’s Inverno 26 campaign, designed for high-impact placement at Santos Dumont Airport. The execution carefully explores video cuts featuring Carol Trentini, balancing her presence with the campaign’s visual language to create an engaging and cohesive experience in an outdoor format.

NINA MORAES DESIGN
CASA MINIMUNDO

Print production and art finalization for two facades of Casa Minimundo in 2023. The project involved adapting the original artwork for large-scale applications, ensuring visual fidelity while meeting the technical requirements of production and installation. As my largest print project at Nina Moraes Design, the work extended beyond the two facades to two additional areas for environment samples, requiring adaptations across different formats, sizes, and materials.
