JOHNNIE WALKER
LOLLAPALOOZA 2026

The objective for Johnnie Walker was to develop the brand’s visual communication for Lollapalooza 2026, establishing a visual direction for the real-time coverage aligned with the brand identity and the dynamics of the festival. The strategy was built around the Go-Go Highball territory and the partnership with Sabrina Carpenter, incorporating its visual codes into the festival communication and creating connections between the product, the Lollapalooza experience, and the brand.

My role as an Art Direction Assistant was focused on the planning and visual direction, working as part of the creative team to establish an approach aligned with the brand and support the development of the content planned for publication throughout the event.

The planning aimed to translate the brand’s identity into a relevant and consistent visual language for the festival, connecting the campaign territory with the Lollapalooza experience. Keep Walking.

CIELO
LOLLAPALOOZA 2026

The objective for Cielo was to establish a visual direction for the Lollapalooza 2026 coverage that would bring the brand closer to the audience’s experience and the energy of the festival. The strategy took Brazilian identity as its starting point, exploring the universe of boteco and gatherings to create a relaxed, diverse, playful, and warm communication.

My role as an Art Direction Assistant, working as part of the creative team, involved contributing to the development of the visual concept and defining the aesthetic direction for the real-time coverage content.

The planning aimed to transform everyday elements of Brazilian culture into a contemporary and spontaneous visual language, connecting Cielo to the moments of celebration, connection, and fun experienced throughout the festival.
