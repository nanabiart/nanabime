JOHNNIE WALKER
LOLLAPALOOZA 2026

The objective for Johnnie Walker was to develop the brand’s visual communication for Lollapalooza 2026, creating a real-time coverage aligned with its identity and the festival’s dynamics. The strategy brought the Go-Go Highball territory and Sabrina Carpenter partnership into the festival context, connecting the product, the experience, and the brand.

CIELO
LOLLAPALOOZA 2026

The objective for Cielo was to establish a visual direction for Lollapalooza 2026, bringing the brand closer to the audience and the energy of the festival. The strategy explored Brazilian identity through the universe of boteco and gatherings, creating a relaxed, diverse, playful, and warm visual language.

My role as an Art Direction Assistant focused on planning and visual direction, contributing to the development of the visual concept alongside the creative team. The planning transformed everyday elements of Brazilian culture into a contemporary and spontaneous language connected to the festival experience.
